Privacy information

Data and permissions

The app uses location permission to show your position, chainage and distance to the imported road alignment. Precise location permission is required for GNSS satellite readings. Location tracking starts when you tap “Show location” and does not run in the background.

The app does not create accounts or send your coordinates to its own server. Alignment, boundary and point files, as well as manually entered target points, are processed and stored on your device. Removing them in the app or deleting the app data removes them from local storage. When you tap “Share coordinates”, your location text is passed to the app you choose.

When you tap “Show driving route”, your current location and selected point coordinates are sent to the FOSSGIS OSRM service to calculate a route. The map background is downloaded from OpenStreetMap; map tile requests may reveal the area viewed and your IP address. In a browser, the DWG reader is downloaded the first time you open a DWG file. Without internet access, location and alignment calculations still work, but map tiles and driving routes are unavailable.
